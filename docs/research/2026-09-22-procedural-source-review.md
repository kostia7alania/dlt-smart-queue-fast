# Procedural source review: first licence, renewal and conversion

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-22

**Scope:** B03 launch review of first-licence, renewal, conversion, document,
fee, expiry and timing claims

**Method:** current web search and page extraction, followed by claim-by-claim
comparison with the repository content. No form, login or appointment request
was submitted.

## What changed since the July review

The 2026-07-31 review correctly recorded that direct requests to DLT pages
returned a JavaScript loading shell. Direct extraction remains inconsistent,
but the official DLT English search surface now returns readable content for:

- a foreigner's new temporary two-year licence, page dated 2025-11-14;
- a foreigner's renewal from a two-year or five-year licence, page dated
  2024-11-20.

This is enough to cite the official pages as dated guidance while keeping their
page age and applicant scope visible. It is not enough to promise that every
branch applies them identically.

## Official source findings

### New temporary licence for a foreigner

The [official English DLT page](https://www.dlt.go.th/en/new-license/20)
publishes:

- passport with visa;
- current Thai address evidence from an embassy or Immigration Bureau, or a
  work permit that identifies the address;
- an original medical certificate;
- document check, physical test, five hours of training, theory test, practical
  test, payment, photograph and licence printing;
- a temporary two-year licence, with fees of 205 baht for car and 105 baht for
  motorcycle.

The page does not settle office-specific copy counts, accepted translations,
test language, payment method or whether another application route fits a
particular person better.

### Renewal for a foreigner

The [official English DLT renewal page](https://www.dlt.go.th/en/two-year-license/42)
publishes separate coverage for a two-year-to-five-year renewal and an existing
five-year renewal. It lists the existing licence, a passport with non-immigrant
visa, current address evidence, and an original five-disease medical form from
a clinic or hospital issued within one month.

The page lists document checking, application printing, physical testing,
payment, photograph and printing, a 505 baht fee, and 50 baht extra for a name
or address change. Its expiry remarks add a theory test after more than one
year and both theory and practical tests after more than three years. It does
not publish a motorcycle renewal figure on the extracted English page.

### June 2026 physical-test and online-renewal state

The [Government Public Relations Department report](https://narathiwat.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/9/iid/514537),
published 2026-06-20 and quoting the DLT spokesman, states that colour-vision
testing remains for a first application but is removed for renewal. It also
describes the age and expiry conditions for the foot-reaction test.

The same report says the fully electronic renewal system was still being
developed and that DLT would announce it after completion. No later official
launch announcement was found in the bounded 2026-09-22 search. The product
therefore states only that the latest official publication found still
described development; it does not claim that absence from search proves the
service remains unavailable.

### Foreign-licence conversion

The current English DLT search did not surface a resident conversion checklist.
The DLT legal library still exposes an [older Thai flow sheet](https://legal.dlt.go.th/gen2/storage/attachments/316_236.pdf)
for tourist licence conversion. It lists passport, the matching home-country
licence, identity and document checks, a physical test, one hour of training,
fee collection and issue.

Because the sheet is roughly a decade old and scoped to tourists, the product
labels it as archived official guidance and does not generalize it into the
current resident procedure. The current conversion checklist, visa acceptance,
translation rules and test waivers remain branch-level questions.

The following specific claims were re-read without promoting them to official
guidance:

  the product now says so explicitly.

  and office variability remain visible.

## Editorial decisions

1. Add `official` as a fourth evidence kind. It always carries publisher, URL
   and access date.
2. Do not use `official` for a search result, commercial summary or personal
   experience merely because it discusses DLT.
3. Keep office-specific acceptance, applicant eligibility and unexercised
   online flows under `official-only`.
4. Keep the archived conversion flow visible only with its age and tourist
   scope.
5. Re-read dates are updated only for the specific source claims checked in
   this review; other third-party claims remain due.
